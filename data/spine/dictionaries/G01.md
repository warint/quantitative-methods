## Traps in this file

> Read these before you model. Each one has cost somebody a week.

- **`share_fossil`** — The three shares sum to 1: including all three plus an intercept is exact collinearity. Drop one.
- **`elec_price_eur_kwh`** — Band IC (500–2000 MWh). A price without its band is meaningless. Structural break in 2022.
- **`elec_price_incl_tax`** — The difference from the excl.-tax series is the policy wedge.
- **`elec_services_gwh`** — The best available proxy for data-centre load, and a poor one.
- **`flag`** — u = low reliability · c = confidential (value is NaN) · b = break in series · p = provisional. Dropping 'u' silently biases coverage toward large economies.
